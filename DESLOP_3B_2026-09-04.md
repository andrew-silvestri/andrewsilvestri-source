### Part 1, second pass — the marginalia collided with the figures

You were right, and it was the 2026-08-30 finding 4 back in HTML: `--wide`
is text plus both gutters, every `.wide` figure ran through the marginalia's
track, and `z-index: 2` painted the notes on top with a `span 60` that kept
it happening for sixty rows. The clamp that fixed it lived in
`margin-scene.js`, which 3a deleted.

**Chosen: (b), made explicit in the grid.** The seven pages with marginalia
carry `<main class="notes">` and a grid of their own: `[full] 1fr ·
[gutter-l] · [wide-start text-start] text [text-end wide-end] · [gutter-r] ·
1fr [full]`. The wide track *is* the text track there, so figures stop at the
column and the two gutter tracks are named lines only the asides use.
Nothing can paint over anything because nothing shares a track. The four
prose pages keep the old grid and full breakout. (a) was rejected on
arithmetic: the outer tracks are 150 px, 18 characters of 12 px mono, and
the strings do not fit. Dropping marginalia on figure-heavy pages was
rejected because the figures are re-rendered in Part 2 at the width they
are displayed at, so a 714 px figure with type sized for 714 px loses
nothing a 1,140 px one had — that is the F3 rule already on the books.

**No magic span.** Each aside now carries `style="grid-row: span N"` where
N is the number of `main` children that follow it, counted from the markup
at build time (`_deslop/phase3b1b.py`: index 18, heat 31, storage 28,
climate-cost 27, longevity 50, skyline 18, desktop 21). No `z-index`.

**The strings.** Every `label: value` item is split at the colon into a label
line (ink) and a value line (dim), and a value never breaks between a
number and its unit (`5.13&nbsp;dollars/MMBtu`). The gutter is 192 px
after padding, about 26 characters; the longest value line is 19. Items
without a colon ("beef, Brazil to Texas") are untouched and may wrap at a
space, which is a wrap between words, not inside a unit.

**Below 1100 px** the gutters fall under 140 px, so the asides sit above
the prose as a two-column list; at 390 px unchanged from 3a.

### One place for the figure style: `sitefig.py`

`sitefig.py` at the repo root holds the palette (the Yacht club tokens and
the series colours derived from them), the face (`fonts/` carries IBM Plex
Sans Regular/Italic/SemiBold and Plex Mono, OFL, registered with matplotlib
by `sitefig.fonts()`), the type scale (`FS_2 … FS2` = 12 / 14.5 / 17.5 / 21 /
25, the same steps as `style.css`), the display widths (`NOTES` 714 for the
seven marginalia pages, `PROSE` 1140, `CARD` 1082 for a library card,
`THUMB` 542), `fig_size()`, `save()` and `thumbnail()`.

**The geometry rule.** `fig_size(display_px, aspect)` makes a figure exactly
`display_px / 72` inches wide, so one point is one CSS pixel: a label set at
`FS_1` is 14.5 px on screen, the same as the caption under it. `save()`
writes at 144 dpi (two bitmap pixels per CSS pixel, for retina) and then
quantises to an 8-bit palette when that is smaller, which for a chart it
always is. `fig_floor.py`'s rule, on-screen px = pt × display / (72 × inches),
still runs in every builder's `audit()`; with this sizing it reduces to pt,
and its floor is now the scale's smallest step (12 px; 14.5 for a title).

**Twelve builders route through it**, patched by `_deslop/phase3b2_builders.py`
(every substitution asserted; the pre-3b builders are in
`backups/builders-pre-3b/`): `build_atlas_figures.py`, `build_throughlines.py`,
`build_model_chart.py`, `build_propagation_diagram.py`, `build_layer_diagram.py`,
`build_allocation_chain.py`, `heat/figstyle.py` + `model.py`,
`storage/figstyle.py` + `model.py`, `longevity-quotient/build_lq.py` and
`fig_lq_explained.py`. Grep on all twelve: **0 hex literals**; every
`fontsize=` / `labelsize=` / `size=` is a scale token; every `figsize=` is a
`fig_size()` call; every save goes through `sitefig.save`. The 30 old site
hexes are mapped to roles once, in `_deslop/palettes.py`. Not converted:
`build_climate_figures.py` (its two outputs are the orphaned
`nitrogen_fixation.png` and `running_shoe.png`, referenced by no page) and
`build_atlas_space.py` (writes no figure).

**Every builder reports 0 layout problems.** Getting there took fourteen
per-figure geometry edits, because 12–14.5 px labels at 714 px do not fit

**Titles.** Decided: a figure carries none. The page heading above it and

a row chart, at 24 CSS px a row; a sheet composes its own. Every
`fig_size()` call now passes one of these (or a stated number for a sheet),
so the next figure inherits a choice, not a default. The heat break-even is
