## 6. Effect size in plain terms

    Two answers to two different questions. Which one the page
    quotes depends on which question it is asking.

    TOTAL - threat's whole advantage, attention left in the pathway
    (the no-views specification):
      NT vs LC:  exp(+0.2874) = 1.33x
      VU vs LC:  exp(+0.5511) = 1.74x
      EN vs LC:  exp(+0.7102) = 2.03x
      CR vs LC:  exp(+0.9738) = 2.65x

    DIRECT - threat's advantage with attention held fixed
    (the full specification):
      NT vs LC:  exp(+0.2411) = 1.27x
      VU vs LC:  exp(+0.4376) = 1.55x
      EN vs LC:  exp(+0.5620) = 1.75x
      CR vs LC:  exp(+0.6416) = 1.90x

## 7. The share of threat's advantage running through attention

    (total - direct) / total, per category. ASSUMED, see below.

    cat     total     direct    through attention
    NT     +0.2874    +0.2411            16.1%
    VU     +0.5511    +0.4376            20.6%
    EN     +0.7102    +0.5620            20.9%
    CR     +0.9738    +0.6416            34.1%

    The share rises monotonically with severity. The more
    endangered the bird, the more of its research advantage runs
    through public attention - and rated attractiveness, the proxy
    this project is named for, carries none of it (dR2 0.0008).

    ASSUMED - the direction. This is a decomposition from a
    cross-section with a single-timepoint mediator, so it cannot
    order the two. threat -> attention -> research and
    research -> better Wikipedia article -> views produce identical
    numbers. The percentages are what the model attributes, not a
    demonstrated sequence, and the word 'mediates' does not appear
    on the page without 'assumed' beside it.
